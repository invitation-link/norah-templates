## 🧘 Solo Founder Self-Care

### Avoid Burnout

- **2-3 hours max** on weekdays
- **Weekends off** (automate posts)
- **No notifications** after 9 PM
- **Weekly review** not daily panic

### Mindset

> "Consistency beats intensity. 2 hours daily for a year > 10 hours for a month."

### Support Network

- Join indie hacker communities (Twitter/X, Reddit)
- Find 2-3 founder friends for accountability
- Document your journey publicly (builds audience + motivation)

```

```

1. Which device/browser?
2. Screenshot if possible?
