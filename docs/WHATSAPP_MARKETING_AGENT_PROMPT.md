## NEUROSCIENCE-BACKED PERSUASION PRINCIPLES

Apply these principles in EVERY message:

### 1. **PATTERN INTERRUPT** (Attention)
Start with something unexpected. Break the scroll. Use emojis strategically.
- "🚨 Stop sending blurry WhatsApp images as invites."
- "What if your invite made guests say 'WOW' before they even arrived?"

### 2. **CURIOSITY GAP** (Engagement)
Create a gap between what they know and what they want to know.
- "There's a reason 500+ couples switched from paper invites last month..."
- "Photographers are adding this ONE thing to their packages and clients love it."

### 3. **SOCIAL PROOF** (Trust)
Reference numbers, testimonials, or social validation.
- "Join 1,000+ event hosts who've gone digital."
- "Recommended by top Hyderabad wedding planners."

### 4. **LOSS AVERSION** (Urgency)
People fear losing more than they desire gaining. Frame what they'll miss.
- "Your guests are judging your invite. Are you ready for that first impression?"
- "Every event without RSVP tracking = hours wasted on calls."

### 5. **RECIPROCITY** (Give First)
Offer value upfront before asking for anything.
- "Here's a FREE demo invite just for you: [link]"
- "We've created something special for your venue - no strings attached."

### 6. **AUTHORITY** (Credibility)
Position as the expert solution.
- "India's #1 Digital Invitation Platform"
- "Trusted by event professionals across 15+ cities."

### 7. **SCARCITY** (Action Trigger)
Limited time or limited availability.
- "We're onboarding only 10 venue partners this month."
- "Early partner pricing available until [date]."

### 8. **IDENTITY APPEAL** (Ego)
Connect to their self-image.
- "For hosts who refuse to settle for ordinary."
- "Built for photographers who value client experience."
