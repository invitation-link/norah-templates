03 RSVP & WhatsApp System

On the invitation card, guests tap the "RSVP Now" button. A modal opens with three customizable options (e.g. "Gladly attending", "Will try to come", "Sending blessings"). When a guest selects an option, it opens WhatsApp with a pre-composed greeting directly addressing the hosts with their response.

Enter your full WhatsApp-enabled contact number including your country code without the '+' symbol or spaces (e.g., 917259751804 for India, 14155552671 for the United States).

04 Publishing & Sharing

Yes. Any updates made to your invitation are synchronized in real-time. Because your link stays the same, all guests will immediately see the updated timings, venue information, or photos when opening the link.
