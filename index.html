What is an interactive invitation?

A shareable web experience instead of a static image or video. Guests open the link in their browser and move through a page designed for the occasion.

Can I send it on WhatsApp?

Yes. Once published, send the same lightweight link to individuals or groups. Guests can open it without installing anything.

Can I customise the details?

Yes. Personalise the names, timing, venue, maps, colours, photos, music, messages and RSVP choices available in the selected template.
