Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026 · GDPR & DPDP Act 2023 Compliant

At Invite Link (accessible at https://www.invitelink.shop), we prioritize the privacy and security of our event hosts and their guests. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal information we collect, our lawful basis for processing, how your data is protected, and your statutory rights under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

1. Data We Collect

  • Host Profile & Event Data: Event names, dates, times, venue addresses, custom quotes, hosts' contact phone numbers, and host email addresses provided during invitation creation and checkout.
  • Media Uploads: Event photos, custom audio clips, and celebration media uploaded into invitations.
  • Guest Interactions & RSVPs: Guest attendance responses, names, guest counts, and dietary notes submitted through interactive RSVP forms.
  • Technical & Analytical Information: Anonymized IP addresses, browser types, device screen resolutions, and performance metrics collected solely for rendering optimization and security logging.

2. Lawful Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Contractual Necessity: To generate, host, render, and maintain your invitation URLs and deliver RSVP routing to the host.
  • Legitimate Interests: To prevent fraud, protect infrastructure against automated abuse, and optimize platform speed.
  • Consent: For non-essential analytics cookies and customer support communications. You may withdraw consent at any time.

3. Trusted Subprocessors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We utilize enterprise infrastructure partners bound by strict Data Processing Agreements (DPAs):

  • Vercel Inc. (USA / Global): Cloud hosting, serverless edge compute, and global CDN caching.
  • Supabase Inc. (AWS Data Centers): Encrypted PostgreSQL database storage and secure media bucket storage.
  • Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. (India): Payment tokenization, UPI/card processing (Invite Link never stores raw card or banking credentials).

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Published invitations and guest responses remain securely stored for the duration of the event hosting period (1 year by default). Hosts may request early permanent deletion of any published invitation, photo asset, or database record at any time by emailing support@invitelink.shop.

5. Your Statutory Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess the right to:

  • Access & Portability: Request a full copy of your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Rectification: Correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Request immediate permanent deletion of your data and media files.
  • Restriction & Objection: Limit or object to specific processing of your personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, please email our Data Protection Officer at support@invitelink.shop. We fulfill verified requests within 30 calendar days.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Invite Link uses essential session storage to preserve your draft invitation edits across page reloads. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers.

7. Contact Our Data Protection Team

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@invitelink.shop or via our Contact Page.
