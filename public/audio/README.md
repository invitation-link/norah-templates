# National Anthem audio

`jana-gana-mana.mp3` is a 52-second vocal recording of “Jana Gana Mana”. It is the MP3 transcode of the public-domain Wikimedia Commons file that identifies the former National Portal of India anthem page as its source.

- Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jana_gana_mana_vocal.ogg
- Official anthem guidance: https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/NationalAnthem%28E%29_2.pdf

Keep the recording uncut and do not place commercial calls to action over anthem playback.
