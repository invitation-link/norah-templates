The short answer

What should the invitation say?

A Varalakshmi Vratham invitation should warmly request the guest's presence and blessings, then give the date, puja time, prasadam or lunch time, hosts' names, address and RSVP details. Keep the wording reverent, direct and easy to scan.

Example opening

With the blessings of Goddess Varalakshmi, we invite you and your family to join us for Varalakshmi Vratham and share in the prayers and prasadam.
