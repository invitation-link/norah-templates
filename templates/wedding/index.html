The short answer

What is an interactive wedding invitation?

An interactive wedding invitation is a shareable web link that lets guests open a designed experience on their phone instead of downloading a static image or large video. It can bring together your event schedule, venue map, countdown, music and RSVP in one place.

  1. Choose your designStart with a wedding style that reflects the mood of your celebration.
  2. Make every detail yoursAdd names, ceremonies, timings, venue, colours, photos and music.
  3. Preview before publishingExperience the invitation as your guests will see it on their phones.
  4. Share one simple linkSend it on WhatsApp—there is no app or attachment for guests to download.

Wedding invitation FAQ

Questions, answered simply

Can I share my wedding invitation on WhatsApp?

Yes. Publish the invitation, copy its link and send it in a WhatsApp message or group. Guests open it in their browser.

Can one invitation include several wedding functions?

Yes. Wedding templates can include a multi-event itinerary for ceremonies such as mehndi, sangeet, wedding and reception.

Do guests need to install an app?

No. The invitation opens from its link in a modern mobile browser.

Can guests RSVP from the invitation?

Yes. The invitation can send a pre-written RSVP response to your WhatsApp number.
